Your roundup on Europe's Deep Tech: Updates on the latest breakthroughs, funding news, and emerging innovation trends.

Weekly Digest — 1–8 June 2026
This week in European deep tech: London's physics AI breaks the $2B ceiling, Finland's quantum unicorn heads for a Nasdaq dual-listing, the UK bets £1.1B on homegrown AI chips, Europe's biggest-ever biotech funding round closes, and London Tech Week opens its doors to 30,000+ innovators. Here are 10 updates from the ecosystem 👇
  1. 🚀
    PhysicsX: Closed a $300M Series C led by Singapore's Temasek, with Nvidia, Siemens, Applied Materials, Atomico, and General Catalyst. The London-based physics AI startup — simulating jet engines, semiconductors, and aerospace hardware — doubled year-on-year revenue and hit a $2.4B valuation.
  2. ⚛️
    IQM (Finland): Upsized its SPAC PIPE to $146M with backing from Finnish pension giant Ilmarinen, targeting a dual listing on Nasdaq and Helsinki Stock Exchange at a $1.8B pre-money valuation. IQM becomes the first European quantum hardware firm to pursue a US public listing.
  3. 💻
    UK Government: Unveiled a £1.1B AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week — £750M for a national AI supercomputer, £150M in chip procurement commitments, and a strategic Arm partnership. The plan targets sovereign AI compute infrastructure amid US-China chip competition.
  4. 🧬
    Isomorphic Labs: Raised $2.1B in a Series B — Europe's largest-ever biotech funding round. The UK DeepMind spinout behind AlphaFold3 is accelerating AI-driven drug discovery across oncology, rare diseases, and immunology.
  5. 🤖
    Agile Robots: The Munich-based industrial robotics giant entered talks for an $800M round with SoftBank as anchor investor, financing the next capital-intensive phase of factory automation and humanoid systems expansion.
  6. ☁️
    EU Tech Sovereignty Package: Proposed on 3 June, the Commission's package includes the Cloud & AI Development Act (CADA), Chips Act 2.0, and an open-source strategy — Europe's most comprehensive push yet to reduce its 80%+ dependence on non-European digital products.
  7. ⚡
    OQC (Oxford Quantum Circuits): Closed £260M in a Series C — Europe's largest-ever private quantum funding round. The British Business Bank committed £100M; Bullhound Capital led. The capital accelerates OQC's superconducting TITAN platform toward fault-tolerant operations.
  8. 🛰️
    ICEYE: Secured a €300M revolving credit facility to scale sovereign space intelligence across Europe. The Finnish SAR satellite firm, now with 62 satellites in orbit, will use it to support customer guarantees and continued fleet growth.
  9. 🇫🇷
    France: President Macron announced €1.55B in new public investment — €1B for quantum technologies and €550M for microelectronics — bringing France's total quantum commitment to €2.9B since 2021, the largest in Europe. Alice & Bob also secured NVIDIA venture backing for its hybrid quantum-GPU collaboration.
  10. 🌍
    London Tech Week 2026: Opened 8 June at Olympia London with a dedicated Deep Tech Stage across AI, quantum, space, robotics, and life sciences. Over 30,000 participants from 130+ countries, 400+ speakers including Perplexity AI's Aravind Srinivas and Isomorphic Labs' Max Jaderberg, with sessions centring on Europe's €4–24B annual growth-stage funding gap.

PhysicsX raises $300M at $2.4B valuation — London's physics AI startup doubles revenue, wins Temasek, Nvidia, Siemens and Applied Materials

UK unveils £1.1B AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week — £750M national supercomputer, £150M chip purchase, and Arm strategic partnership

Technology Secretary Liz Kendall announced the UK's £1.1 billion AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week, anchored by a £750M investment in a new national AI supercomputer (deploying by 2030, including £400M in next-generation chips) and a £150M advance commitment to buy chips from British startups. A new £150M fund backed by the British Business Bank and led by Playground Global will invest in UK hardware companies. The plan also establishes a Centre for Doctoral Training in Chip Design and a strategic partnership with Arm.

OQC raises £260M ($350M) Series C — Europe's largest-ever private quantum computing round to scale superconducting QC into data centres globally

Oxford Quantum Circuits (OQC) has closed an oversubscribed £260 million Series C led by Bullhound Capital, with the British Business Bank committing £100M and Mastercard, Fynveur (Invus), COFIDES, Magdalen College Oxford, and Pentland Ventures among co-investors. European Commission POLICY 3 Jun 2026

EU Tech Sovereignty Package PROPOSED — Cloud & AI Development Act (CADA), Chips Act 2.0, and open-source strategy target US Big Tech in government contracts

The European Commission unveiled its landmark Tech Sovereignty Package on 3 June 2026, its most comprehensive move yet to reduce the EU's 70%+ reliance on US cloud platforms. The Cloud and AI Development Act (CADA) creates four "assurance levels" for cloud sovereignty — barring providers that fail Level 3 (EU-owned and controlled) from sensitive government contracts in banking, energy, and healthcare. The package also includes Chips Act 2.0 to cut the EU's dependence on non-European semiconductors (currently <10% of global production) and a goal to triple EU data-centre capacity by 2032.
