TechFundingNews DEFENCE / AI 29 May 2026

Helsing closes $1.2B at $18B valuation — Germany's most valuable startup ever, backed by Lightspeed and Dragoneer

Munich-based Helsing has officially closed its $1.2 billion round led by Lightspeed and Dragoneer, cementing its position as Germany's highest-valued startup and Europe's leading defence AI company. The raise follows Helsing's €600M round from Daniel Ek just a year ago and brings total funding past $2B. Helsing builds AI software and systems across air, land, sea, and underwater operations for European governments — built on a sovereign, non-US-cloud architecture.

Tech.eu DEFENCE / SPACE 27 May 2026

Helsing and OHB form KIRK — AI-powered space targeting and tactical reconnaissance joint venture for European defence

Helsing and OHB, Europe's leading space group, have established a joint venture named KIRK (Künstliche Intelligenz und Raumfahrt-Kompetenz) alongside Kongsberg Defence and HENSOLDT to build space-based tactical surveillance and AI-driven targeting systems. The satellites will be fully “software-defined”, enabling them to dynamically counter new threats through AI onboard optimisation. The JV signals Europe's growing ambition to combine sovereign AI with sovereign space capabilities in a single integrated defence stack.

Tech.eu AI / RESEARCH 13 May 2026

Recursive Superintelligence raises $650M from GV, Nvidia, and AMD — DeepMind spinout bets self-improving AI can reach superintelligence

London-based Recursive Superintelligence has emerged from stealth with a $650M raise led by GV (Google's VC arm) and Greycroft, with chip giants Nvidia and AMD participating, valuing the company at $4.65B. Founded by former DeepMind researchers, the startup is building AI systems that improve themselves by analysing their own performance — without human intervention. The raise makes it Europe's third-largest AI frontier lab raise this year, alongside Ineffable Intelligence ($1.1B) and Advanced Machine Intelligence ($1B).

Sifted AI / INDUSTRIAL 28 May 2026

Mistral AI launches Industrial Engineering AI with Airbus, BMW, EDF, and CMA CGM — Europe's physical AI race is on

French AI champion Mistral has formally launched Mistral for Industrial Engineering, signing Airbus, BMW, EDF, and CMA CGM as headline customers. Airbus deploys it across its commercial, helicopter, and space divisions; BMW uses it to accelerate crash simulations; EDF for nuclear plant engineering. The move anchors Mistral's physical-AI commercial strategy and deepens Europe's push to build sovereign industrial AI outside US cloud platforms.

TechFundingNews ROBOTICS / AI 4 Mar 2026

NEURA Robotics raises €1B from Tether — German humanoid robot maker valued at €4B as physical AI bets surge

Metzingen-based NEURA Robotics closed a €1 billion round backed by Tether Holdings, Amazon, and Qualcomm, valuing the company at €4 billion. The raise makes NEURA Europe's best-funded humanoid robotics startup and follows a partnership with Amazon Web Services to deploy NEURA robots in fulfillment centres. Humanoid robotics is emerging as one of Europe's fastest-growing deep tech verticals, with NEURA competing directly with Boston Dynamics and Figure AI on the global stage.

European Commission POLICY 27 May 2026

EU Tech Sovereignty Package ADOPTED — Cloud & AI Development Act, Chips Act 2.0, and open-source strategy now official EU law

The European Commission formally adopted its landmark Tech Sovereignty Package on 27 May 2026 — the bloc's most comprehensive attempt to reduce its 80%+ dependence on non-European digital products. The package bundles the Cloud and AI Development Act (CAIDA), defining sovereign cloud standards, Chips Act 2.0, an EU open-source strategy, and a digital infrastructure roadmap. The package was delayed twice and arrives amid escalating concerns over US cloud data sovereignty.
