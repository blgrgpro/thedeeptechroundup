TechCrunch DEFENCE / AI 11 May 2026

Helsing nears $1.2B raise at $18B valuation — set to become Germany's most valuable startup ever

Munich-based defence AI startup Helsing is closing a $1.2 billion round led by Dragoneer Investment Group and Lightspeed, valuing it at $18 billion — making it Germany's most valuable startup ever. Founded in 2021, Helsing has expanded from AI battlefield-data analysis into loitering munitions (the HX-2, 100km range, onboard AI targeting). The Bundestag approved a €269M HX-2 contract in February, scaleable to €1.46B over seven years. The latest round was oversubscribed multiple times, reflecting surging investor appetite for European defence tech.

EU Startups QUANTUM 5 May 2026

QuantWare raises €152M — the largest-ever private round for a dedicated quantum processor company

Dutch quantum hardware startup QuantWare raised €152 million ($178M) in a Series B round led by Intel Capital and ETF Partners — the largest private equity raise ever by a dedicated quantum processor company. Funds will build KiloFab, the world's largest dedicated open-architecture quantum fab, boosting production capacity by 20x. Spun out of QuTech, QuantWare has shipped quantum processing units to 50+ customers in 20 countries and unveiled the VIO-40K™ architecture targeting 10,000 qubits — 100x larger than current state of the art.

European Commission POLICY 20 May 2026

EU Tech Sovereignty Package due 27 May — Chips Act 2, Cloud & AI Act, and open-source strategy in scope

The European Commission is set to unveil its long-awaited Tech Sovereignty Package on 27 May 2026, covering a Cloud and AI Development Act, Chips Act 2.0, an EU open-source software strategy, and a digital infrastructure roadmap — the bloc's most comprehensive attempt to reduce its 80%+ dependence on non-European digital products. Delayed twice from earlier 2026 dates due to lobbying pressure from US tech firms and internal coordination challenges across member states, the package is now eagerly awaited by Europe's tech ecosystem.

EIC RESEARCH FUNDING 20 May 2026

EIC Pathfinder 2026 closes with record 2,103 proposals requesting €8.2B — demand for EU breakthrough science surges

The European Innovation Council's flagship open science call closed on 12 May with 2,103 project proposals from 76 countries collectively requesting over €8.2 billion — a significant year-on-year increase. The oversubscription signals surging global appetite for EU-backed high-risk research at the frontier of quantum, biotech, AI, and advanced materials. Evaluations are now underway; results expected October 2026.
