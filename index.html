Sifted VENTURE CAPITAL 4 Feb 2026

Kembara closes €750M first tranche of €1B deep tech growth fund — Europe's largest dedicated deep tech vehicle

Spain-based Kembara secured a €750M first close toward a €1B target, backed by the European Investment Fund with a €350M anchor commitment, targeting €15–40M per company across AI, quantum, robotics, clean energy, space, and dual-use technologies. The fund, co-founded by Mundi Ventures CEO Javier Santiso and ex-Atomico partner Yann de Vries, addresses the critical gap where 97% of European deep tech funds remain below €300M — too small to lead the growth rounds these capital-intensive companies require.

Tech.eu QUANTUM 7 May 2026

Quantum Motion raises $160M Series C to deliver quantum computing's "transistor moment" on standard silicon

London-based Quantum Motion, founded by UCL and Oxford professors, closed a $160M Series C co-led by DCVC and Kembara, with the British Business Bank contributing a £40M anchor — its largest-ever single quantum investment. The company's silicon CMOS architecture promises 100× cost reduction and 1,000× lower energy consumption versus competing approaches, and already has a working unit installed at the UK's national quantum facility.

The Next Web AI 27 Apr 2026

Ex-DeepMind legend David Silver closes $1.1B seed at $5.1B valuation — Europe's largest-ever seed round

Ineffable Intelligence, Silver's London AI lab, raised a record-breaking $1.1B seed led by Sequoia and Lightspeed with Nvidia, Google, and Index as co-investors, targeting an AI that learns without human-generated data through pure reinforcement learning. Silver argues that large language models are fundamentally capped by the human data they are trained on, and his "superlearner" approach aims to exceed that ceiling entirely — building AI that can discover genuinely new knowledge.

TechCrunch QUANTUM / IPO 15 Mar 2026

Pasqal targets $2B SPAC listing on Nasdaq — Europe's first quantum computing public market offering

French quantum computing startup Pasqal, which builds neutral-atom quantum processors, is pursuing a $2B SPAC merger on Nasdaq — a landmark moment that would make it the first European quantum company to list on a major US exchange. The move underscores the tension at the heart of EU tech sovereignty: Europe's quantum champions are looking to US capital markets while Brussels prepares sovereign financing instruments that may arrive too late.
