PhysicsX raises $300M at $2.4B valuation — London's physics AI startup doubles revenue, wins Temasek, Nvidia, Siemens and Applied Materials

London-based PhysicsX has closed an oversubscribed $300M Series C led by Singapore's Temasek, with Nvidia, Siemens, Applied Materials, Atomico, and General Catalyst participating. The raise values the company — which builds AI models that simulate physical behaviour in jet engines, semiconductors, and aerospace hardware — at $2.4 billion. PhysicsX doubled year-on-year revenue and tripled booked revenue in the past 12 months, and has grown to more than 300 people.

GOV.UK AI / POLICY 8 Jun 2026

UK unveils £1.1B AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week — £750M national supercomputer, £150M chip purchase, and Arm strategic partnership

Technology Secretary Liz Kendall announced the UK's £1.1 billion AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week, anchored by a £750M investment in a new national AI supercomputer (deploying by 2030, including £400M in next-generation chips) and a £150M advance commitment to buy chips from British startups. A new £150M fund backed by the British Business Bank and led by Playground Global will invest in UK hardware companies. The plan also establishes a Centre for Doctoral Training in Chip Design and a strategic partnership with Arm.

Eurosatory 2026 opens in Paris — largest defence exhibition in history; 2,600+ exhibitors from 68 nations, AI drones and autonomous systems dominate

Eurosatory 2026 opened today at Paris-Nord Villepinte — the largest defence and security exhibition in the event's 60-year history, with more than 2,600 exhibitors from 68 countries filling 185,000 square metres. The Next Web AI / M&A 24 Apr 2026

Cohere acquires Aleph Alpha in $20B merger — creates transatlantic “sovereign AI” powerhouse; Schwarz Digits commits $600M as Germany and Canada back the deal

Cohere, the Canadian enterprise AI company, announced on April 24 it would merge with Germany's Aleph Alpha in a deal valuing the combined entity at $20 billion — the most significant transatlantic AI deal in history. Cohere retains its name and around 90% of the combined entity, operating dual headquarters in Toronto and Heidelberg. Schwarz Digits, the tech arm of Europe's largest private retailer, committed $600M in equity and research funding to the merged company. Both the German and Canadian governments endorsed the deal as a flagship “sovereign AI” play to challenge US hyperscalers in enterprise and public-sector AI markets.

Quantum Insider QUANTUM / INFRASTRUCTURE 3 Jun 2026

OQC raises £260M ($350M) Series C — Europe's largest-ever private quantum computing round to scale superconducting QC into data centres globally

Oxford Quantum Circuits (OQC) has closed an oversubscribed £260 million Series C led by Bullhound Capital, with the British Business Bank committing £100M and Mastercard, Fynveur (Invus), COFIDES, Magdalen College Oxford, and Pentland Ventures among co-investors. The raise is Europe's largest-ever private quantum funding round and will fund OQC's global deployment of superconducting quantum computers in data-centre environments across the UK, US, Japan, and Spain, directly enabling quantum-AI hybrid workloads at enterprise scale.

European Commission POLICY 3 Jun 2026

EU Tech Sovereignty Package PROPOSED — Cloud & AI Development Act (CADA), Chips Act 2.0, and open-source strategy target US Big Tech in government contracts

The European Commission unveiled its landmark Tech Sovereignty Package on 3 June 2026, its most comprehensive move yet to reduce the EU's 70%+ reliance on US cloud platforms. The Cloud and AI Development Act (CADA) creates four "assurance levels" for cloud sovereignty — barring providers that fail Level 3 (EU-owned and controlled) from sensitive government contracts in banking, energy, and healthcare. The package also includes Chips Act 2.0 to cut the EU's dependence on non-European semiconductors (currently <10% of global production) and a goal to triple EU data-centre capacity by 2032.
