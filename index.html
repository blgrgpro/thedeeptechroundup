Quantum Insider QUANTUM / POLICY 22 May 2026

France pledges €1.55B for quantum and microchips — Macron's Plan Quantique hits €2.9B total as France bets on tech sovereignty

President Macron announced €1.55 billion in new investment at the CEA's Very Large Computing Center in Bruyères-le-Châtel — €1B expanding the sovereign Plan Quantique strategy and €550M for advanced microelectronics manufacturing. France's total quantum commitment now stands at €2.9B since 2021, making it the largest state backer of quantum technology in Europe. The announcement came on the same day Alice & Bob, selected under France's PROQCIMA military quantum procurement framework, received new backing from NVIDIA's venture arm.

Sifted VC / POLICY 18 May 2026

EQT beats Atomico to manage EU's €5B Scaleup Europe Fund — Europe's most ambitious attempt to close the growth-stage gap

The European Innovation Council has selected Stockholm-based EQT as fund manager for the €5 billion Scaleup Europe Fund, beating Atomico and other top European firms in a closely-watched competition. The fund targets tech scaleups across AI, quantum, dual-use technologies, clean energy, space, biotech, and medical innovation. Founding investors include Novo Holdings, APG (ABP), Allianz, Santander/Mouro Capital, and Intesa Sanpaolo, with first investments expected in autumn 2026.

Sifted AI / M&A 19 May 2026

Mistral AI acquires Emmi AI — second acquisition in three months signals major industrial AI push

French AI company Mistral has acquired Emmi AI, a Vienna-based physical AI startup founded in 2024 as a spin-out of NXAI. Emmi builds specialised models for industrial engineering workflows — computational fluid dynamics, heat transfer, materials stress testing — and raised the largest-ever Austrian seed round. The deal opens a new Mistral office in Linz and brings 30+ researchers into Mistral's Science and Applied AI teams.

The Recursive EVENTS 20 May 2026

Deep Tech Momentum Berlin 2026 draws 3,000+ leaders to tackle Europe's deep tech commercialisation gap

The annual Deep Tech Momentum conference (20–21 May, Berlin) gathered over 3,000 corporate leaders, investors, and founders across AI, energy, space, defence, and advanced materials. Europe's persistent challenge of building world-class deep tech but failing to scale it at US or Chinese speed was the defining theme, with sessions centring on the €4–24B annual growth-stage funding gap.

European Commission POLICY 20 May 2026

EU Tech Sovereignty Package due 27 May — Chips Act 2, Cloud & AI Act, and open-source strategy in scope

The European Commission is set to unveil its long-awaited Tech Sovereignty Package on 27 May 2026, covering a Cloud and AI Development Act, Chips Act 2.0, an EU open-source software strategy, and a digital infrastructure roadmap — the bloc's most comprehensive attempt to reduce its 80%+ dependence on non-European digital products. The package was delayed twice, reflecting intense lobbying from US tech firms.

EIC RESEARCH FUNDING 20 May 2026

EIC Pathfinder 2026 closes with record 2,103 proposals requesting €8.2B — demand for EU breakthrough science surges

The European Innovation Council's flagship open science call closed on 12 May with 2,103 project proposals from 76 countries collectively requesting over €8.2 billion — a significant year-on-year increase. The oversubscription signals surging global appetite for EU-backed high-risk research at the frontier of quantum, biotech, AI, and advanced materials. Results expected October 2026.
