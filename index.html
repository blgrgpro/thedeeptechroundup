ArcticStartup DEFENCE / ROBOTICS 30 Jun 2026

Six Robotics raises €12M seed to scale Valkyrie drone swarm platform - ex-Special Forces founder targets European defence markets with hardware-agnostic autonomy software

Oslo-based Six Robotics has raised €12 million in a seed equity round led by DTCP, with Denmark's state-backed EIFO and Copenhagen VC Scale Capital participating. Founded in 2023, the company's Valkyrie platform allows a single operator to control a coordinated swarm of drones across manufacturers through shared autonomy, real-time task distribution, and onboard decision-making. The raise comes as European defence buyers rapidly accelerate procurement of software-defined drone systems. Six Robotics will use the capital to expand deployments across European and allied defence markets and scale its engineering team.

Quantum Insider QUANTUM / PHOTONICS 29 Jun 2026

QuTech (Delft) achieves above-unity coherent nanophotonic coupling for diamond SnV qubits - Physical Review X breakthrough clears key quantum networking bottleneck

Researchers at QuTech, the quantum institute at Delft University of Technology, have demonstrated above-unity coherent cooperativity using diamond tin-vacancy (SnV) colour centres in engineered photonic crystal cavities. Published in Physical Review X, the result resolves a long-standing bottleneck in connecting solid-state qubits via photons - the foundation for quantum networks and modular quantum computing. The scalable, CMOS-compatible silicon nitride architecture operates at low noise with reliable qubit-to-photon coupling, significantly advancing Europe's efforts to build a quantum internet.

EU Startups VC / DEEPTECH 26 Jun 2026

EIFO commits €200M to EQT's €5B Scaleup Europe Fund - anchored by €1B from European Commission; Novo Holdings and Allianz join as founding investors

Denmark's national promotional bank EIFO has committed €200 million to the Scaleup Europe Fund, becoming the only national promotional bank among the founding investors. Managed by Swedish private equity giant EQT, the fund targets a total size of €5 billion anchored by a €1 billion European Commission commitment, with Novo Holdings, Allianz, and APG also participating in the first close. The fund will invest in 30–40 European tech scaleups working across AI, quantum technology, robotics, energy tech, space, biotech, medtech, and agritech, with first investments anticipated in autumn 2026.

Sifted AI / ENGINEERING 8 Jun 2026

PhysicsX raises $300M at $2.4B valuation - London's physics AI startup doubles revenue, wins Temasek, Nvidia, Siemens and Applied Materials

London-based PhysicsX has closed an oversubscribed $300M Series C led by Singapore's Temasek, with Nvidia, Siemens, Applied Materials, Atomico, and General Catalyst participating. GOV.UK AI / POLICY 8 Jun 2026

UK unveils £1.1B AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week - £750M national supercomputer, £150M chip purchase, and Arm strategic partnership

Technology Secretary Liz Kendall announced the UK's £1.1 billion AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week, anchored by a £750M investment in a new national AI supercomputer (deploying by 2030, including £400M in next-generation chips) and a £150M advance commitment to buy chips from British startups. A new £150M fund backed by the British Business Bank and led by Playground Global will invest in UK hardware companies. The plan also establishes a Centre for Doctoral Training in Chip Design and a strategic partnership with Arm.

Defense Update DEFENCE / EVENTS 15 Jun 2026

Eurosatory 2026 opens in Paris - largest defence exhibition in history; 2,600+ exhibitors from 68 nations, AI drones and autonomous systems dominate

Eurosatory 2026 opened today at Paris-Nord Villepinte - the largest defence and security exhibition in the event's 60-year history, with more than 2,600 exhibitors from 68 countries filling 185,000 square metres. Over 350 official procurement and government delegations from 100 nations are in attendance through June 19. French Minister of Armed Forces Catherine Vautrin and EU Defence Commissioner Andrius Kubilius presided at the opening ceremony. Autonomous aerial systems, counter-drone technology, and AI battlefield integration dominate the agenda, with Helsing, Rheinmetall, Lockheed Martin, and hundreds of European defence-tech startups showcasing their latest hardware and software.

Axios DEFENCE / AI 9 Jun 2026

US Army tests Helsing HX-2 in Lithuania and hits 15 of 17 targets during Project Flytrap 5.0, sparking US procurement interest in the German AI drone

US Army soldiers tested the German-built Helsing HX-2 AI strike drone during Project Flytrap 5.0, a V Corps-led multinational exercise at Pabrade Training Area on NATO's eastern flank. The electrically powered HX-2 weighs 12 kg, reaches 220 km/h, and has a 100 km range. It achieved 15 confirmed kills and 2 near-misses from 17 engagements (88% hit rate) in a heavily contested electronic warfare environment. The results have impressed US military observers and are expected to accelerate procurement discussions between Washington and Helsing.

The Exploration Company unveils Yrene, a new reusable heavy-lift rocket powered by its Storm engine, as competition in Europe's commercial launch market intensifies

The Exploration Company, the Franco-German startup behind the Nyx cargo spacecraft, has revealed Yrene, a new reusable heavy-lift rocket designed to complement its in-orbit services business. Powered by the company's in-house Storm engine, Yrene targets the growing demand for large-payload delivery to low Earth orbit at competitive costs.
