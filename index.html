Sifted AI / MATERIALS 20 Jul 2026

CuspAI lands $450M Series B at $2.6B valuation — Jeff Bezos-backed Cambridge startup launches AI Materials Foundry backed by NVIDIA, AMD, and Kleiner Perkins

Cambridge-based CuspAI has raised $450 million in a Series B round valuing the company at $2.6 billion, with backing from Jeff Bezos (Bezos Expeditions), Kleiner Perkins, NEA, AMD Ventures, and Britain's Sovereign AI Venture Fund. The company, founded in 2024 by Chad Edwards and Max Welling, builds an AI platform to discover entirely new materials for semiconductors, clean energy, and advanced manufacturing. Alongside the funding, CuspAI launched its AI Materials Foundry initiative with 45 industry partners — including NVIDIA — aiming to cut the time from material concept to commercial application from decades to years.

CNBC DEFENCE / AI 13 Jul 2026

Helsing raises $1.8B at $18B valuation — Europe's largest-ever defence-startup financing led by JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs Alternatives, and Lightspeed

Munich-based defence AI company Helsing has closed a $1.8 billion Series E round at an $18 billion valuation — the largest funding round ever raised by a European defence-technology startup. Investors include JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs Alternatives, Lightspeed Venture Partners, Dragoneer, ICONIQ Growth, Canada Pension Plan Investment Board, General Catalyst, and Plural. Investor demand significantly exceeded the available allocation. Helsing, which builds AI software for combat aircraft, drones, and submarines, generated approximately €300 million in revenue in 2025 and has now raised more than $3 billion in total.

Sifted ROBOTICS / AI 16 Jul 2026

microagi raises $55M in Germany's largest-ever seed round — Munich startup co-founded by F1 engineers trains the next generation of humanoid robots on real-world data

Munich-based robotics startup microagi has closed a $55 million seed round — the largest seed round ever raised by a German company — led by Hummingbird, with Northzone, LocalGlobe, Village Global, and Redalpine participating. Founded just 10 months ago by former Formula 1 aerodynamics engineers from Red Bull Racing and Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1, alongside an Alan Turing Institute researcher and a WhatsApp commerce co-founder, the company collects factory and household data to train humanoid robots at scale. Its consumer arm, shift, offers free professional home cleanings in exchange for first-person-view training footage for robotic AI.

EU Startups FUSION / ENERGY 7 Jul 2026

Proxima Fusion secures €411M in Europe's largest-ever fusion investment — Google and RWE back Munich stellarator startup at €2.4B valuation

Munich-based Proxima Fusion, a Max Planck Institute spinout, has raised €411 million at a €2.4 billion valuation in the largest fusion energy investment ever recorded in Europe. The round was led by XTX Ventures and East X Ventures, with Google and RWE as strategic investors, alongside KfW Capital, SPRIND, Balderton, Cherry Ventures, DST Global, Lightspeed, and others. The funding will build Alpha, the company's net-gain stellarator fusion demonstrator near Munich — the critical bridge between decades of plasma physics research and the first commercial fusion power plant, Stellaris, planned for the site of a former nuclear plant in Bavaria.
