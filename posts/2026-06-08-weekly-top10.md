# The Deep Tech Roundup — Week of 1–8 June 2026

Last week in European deep tech, London's physics AI breaks the $2.4B ceiling, Finland's quantum unicorn heads for a Nasdaq dual-listing, the UK bets £1.1B on homegrown AI chips, Munich's robotics giant eyes an $800M SoftBank round, Oxford locks in Europe's largest-ever private quantum raise, and London Tech Week throws open its doors to 30,000+ innovators. Here are 6 updates from the ecosystem 👇

🚀 **PhysicsX**: Closed a $300M Series C led by Temasek, with NVIDIA, Siemens, Applied Materials, Atomico, and General Catalyst. The London physics AI startup doubled year-on-year revenue and hit a $2.4B valuation.

⚛️ **IQM Quantum Computers**: Upsized its SPAC PIPE to $146M backed by Finnish pension giant Ilmarinen, targeting a dual listing on Nasdaq + Helsinki Stock Exchange at a $1.8B valuation — the first European quantum hardware firm to go public in the US.

💻 **UK Government**: Unveiled a £1.1B AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week today, which includes £750M national AI supercomputer, £150M chip procurement, and an Arm strategic partnership targeting sovereign AI compute.

🤖 **Agile Robots SE**: The Munich industrial robotics giant entered talks for an $800M round with SoftBank as anchor, financing its next phase of factory automation and humanoid systems.

⚡ **Oxford Quantum Circuits (OQC)**: Closed £260M Series C — Europe's largest-ever private quantum funding round. Led by Bullhound Capital with investments from British Business Bank and Oxford Science Enterprises.

🌍 **London Tech Week 2026**: Opened today (8th June) at Olympia London, Deep Tech Stage across AI, quantum, space, robotics, and life sciences. 30,000+ from 130+ countries; sessions centring on Europe's €4–24B growth-stage funding gap.
