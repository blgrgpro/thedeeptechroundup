# The Deep Tech Roundup — Week of 1–8 June 2026

This week in European deep tech: London's physics AI breaks the $2B ceiling, Finland's quantum unicorn heads for a Nasdaq dual-listing, the UK bets £1.1B on homegrown AI chips, Europe's biggest biotech funding round in history closes, and London Tech Week opens its doors to 30,000+ innovators. Here are 10 updates from the ecosystem 👇

🚀 **PhysicsX**: Closed a $300M Series C led by Singapore's Temasek, with Nvidia, Siemens, Applied Materials, Atomico, and General Catalyst. The London-based physics AI startup — which simulates jet engines, semiconductors, and aerospace hardware — doubled year-on-year revenue and hits a $2.4B valuation.

⚛️ **IQM** (Finland): Upsized its SPAC PIPE to $146M with new backing from Finnish pension giant Ilmarinen, setting up a dual listing on Nasdaq and the Helsinki Stock Exchange at a $1.8B pre-money valuation. IQM becomes the first European quantum hardware firm to pursue a US public listing, with up to $477M expected cash post-close.

💻 **UK Government**: Unveiled a £1.1B AI Hardware Plan at London Tech Week — £750M for a national AI supercomputer, £150M in chip procurement commitments, and a strategic partnership with Arm. The plan targets sovereign AI compute infrastructure as US-China chip competition intensifies.

🧬 **Isomorphic Labs**: Raised $2.1B in a Series B, making it Europe's largest-ever biotech funding round. The UK DeepMind spinout behind AlphaFold3 is accelerating AI-driven drug discovery across oncology, rare diseases, and immunology.

🤖 **Agile Robots**: The Munich-based industrial robotics giant entered talks for an $800M funding round with SoftBank as anchor investor. The round would finance the next capital-intensive phase of Agile's expansion into factory automation and humanoid systems.

☁️ **EU Tech Sovereignty Package**: Proposed on 3 June, the Commission's long-awaited package includes the Cloud & AI Development Act (CADA), Chips Act 2.0, and an open-source strategy — Europe's most comprehensive push yet to reduce its 80%+ dependence on non-European digital products and curb US Big Tech dominance in government contracts.

⚡ **OQC** (Oxford Quantum Circuits): Closed £260M in a Series C — Europe's largest-ever private quantum funding round. The British Business Bank committed £100M, with Bullhound Capital leading. The capital accelerates OQC's superconducting TITAN platform toward commercially viable, fault-tolerant operations.

🛰️ **ICEYE**: Secured a €300M revolving credit facility to scale sovereign space intelligence across Europe. The Finnish SAR satellite firm, operating 62 satellites, will use the facility to support customer guarantees and continued fleet growth.

🇫🇷 **France**: President Macron announced €1.55B in new public investment — €1B for quantum technologies and €550M for microelectronics — bringing France's total quantum commitment to €2.9B since 2021, the largest in Europe. Alice & Bob also secured investment from NVentures, NVIDIA's venture arm, deepening its hybrid quantum-GPU collaboration.

🌍 **London Tech Week 2026**: Opened 8 June at Olympia London with a dedicated Deep Tech Stage across AI, quantum, space, robotics, and life sciences. Over 30,000 participants from 130+ countries, 400+ speakers including Perplexity AI's Aravind Srinivas and Isomorphic Labs' Max Jaderberg. NATO Innovation Fund and Amadeus Capital discussed Europe's deeptech capital gap.
